Everything Merx ships is listed in the [reference](/reference). These are the terms that are easy to mix up.

## Payment provider

*Other terms: payment service, PSP*

An external service that handles the payment. For example PayPal or Stripe.

## Payment gateway

The connection between Merx and a payment provider: one function that starts a payment and one that completes it. Merx ships `invoice`, `paypal` and `stripe-elements`, and the checkout form picks one per order with its `paymentGateway` field. See [Payment gateways](/guide/payment-gateways).

## Payment method

What the customer actually pays with — credit card, SEPA direct debit, iDEAL, PayPal balance, invoice. One gateway can offer many: [Stripe Elements](/guide/payment-gateways/stripe-elements) shows whichever methods you enabled in your Stripe account.

## Virtual order page

An [`OrderPage`](/reference/classes/order-page) that does not exist on disk yet. Merx builds it when the checkout is submitted and keeps it in the session while the customer is at the payment provider; only a completed payment turns it into a real page.

This is what a [custom payment gateway](/guide/payment-gateways/custom-payment-gateway) receives, and why it has to be changed with [`$page->version()->update()`](https://getkirby.com/docs/reference/objects/content/version/update) rather than `$page->update()`.

## Discount

A cart item with a negative price and the type `discount`. It is part of the total like any other item, which is why discounts need no special handling. See [Shipping costs and discounts](/cookbook/shipping-costs-and-discounts).

## Coupon

A code a customer enters to get a discount. Merx has no coupon feature — you check the code yourself and add a discount item to the cart when it is valid.
