Title: Configuration

----

Headline: 

----

Intro: Merx works out of the box. What you configure is what makes it your shop: your currency, your tax rates, your payment keys.

----

Text:

Everything on the following pages is set in `/site/config/config.php` under the `wagnerwagner.merx.` prefix. The [config options reference](/reference/options/config-options) lists every option with its default value.

Start with [currencies](/guide/configuration/currencies) and [taxes](/guide/configuration/taxes) — they decide what a price means and are used by everything else. [Stripe/PayPal secrets](/guide/configuration/stripe-paypal-secrets) connects your payment provider, and [testing and production](/guide/configuration/testing-and-production) is what you change last, when the shop goes live.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never commit Stripe or PayPal secrets to a repository. [Stripe/PayPal secrets](/guide/configuration/stripe-paypal-secrets) shows where to keep them instead.
