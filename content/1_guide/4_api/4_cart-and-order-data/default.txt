Title: Cart and order data

----

Headline: 

----

Intro: A cart lives in the visitor’s session, an order in a content file. What is kept where decides what can still change.

----

Text:

## In the session

The cart keeps little of each item: its `key`, the UUID of its page, the `quantity`, the `quantifier`, your `data` and the `type`. Title and price are read from the page with every request, so they follow the Panel and the visitor’s language. An item without a page, such as shipping, has nothing to read them from and keeps its own title and price.

Next to the cart, the session only holds what a checkout needs for a moment: the id of the order it started, the id of a Stripe payment, and the [error](/reference/classes/merx/error) of a payment that failed. No customer data is stored there.

## In the order

When the checkout is submitted, Merx writes the order as a draft page, and publishes it once the payment is completed. It holds:

- **The customer’s data**, as it was entered. Escape it when you [display it](/guide/getting-started/displaying-order).
- **The items**, each with its title and its net and gross price, so the order stays as it was when products change.
- **The language** the customer ordered in, on a site with several.
- **What Merx adds itself**: the order number, the dates it was created and paid, and a short record of the payment. A customer [cannot set these](/guide/security).

A draft of a checkout that was never completed is deleted by the next checkout once it is a week old.
