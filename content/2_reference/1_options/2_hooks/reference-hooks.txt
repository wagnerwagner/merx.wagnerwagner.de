Shortdescription: The points in the cart and checkout where Merx lets you run your own code.

----

Description: A `:before` hook may throw to refuse what is about to happen: a Kirby exception reaches the customer with its message and status code. An `:after` hook runs when the work is done, so throwing there reports an error and undoes nothing. Once an order is written, Merx only logs what [`orderPaid`](/reference/options/hooks/order-paid) and [`createOrder:after`](/reference/options/hooks/create-order-after) throw, and the customer gets their order. Three hooks hand a value back: [`cart.add:before`](/reference/options/hooks/cart-add-before), [`createOrder:after`](/reference/options/hooks/create-order-after) and [`orderPaid`](/reference/options/hooks/order-paid).
