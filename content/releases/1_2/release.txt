Title: Merx 2

----

Headline: More shop. Just as simple.

----

Intro: Merx 2 is the new major version of Merx, built for Kirby 6. Prices in any currency, tax rates per product and country, a JSON API that keeps your pages cacheable, and every Stripe payment method in one integration. It’s a toolset, not a framework — if you know Kirby, you know Merx.
