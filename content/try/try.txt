Title: Try

----

Headline: Try Merx

----

Intro: Test Merx for as long as you like, on your computer or on a staging server. You only need a license once your shop goes public.

----

Starterkit: A complete shop to explore and build on: products, cart, checkout and order pages, with commented blueprints, templates, JavaScript and CSS. Browse the demo shop, switch between Germany, International and USA to see currency and prices change, and go through the checkout in test mode.

----

Plugin: Merx is a plugin, so it fits into the site you already have. Your templates, blueprints and frontend stay yours, and Merx adds the cart, the checkout and the orders. Install it with Composer, as a Git submodule or as a download.

----

Showcase: Chocolate makers, jewelers, photographers and fashion labels sell with Merx.

----
